# Validation

Date: 2026-10-18. tcc: 0.9.28rc. evm (go-ethereum): 1.14.12. Host
executable: `build/langc` at 8dd82a3 plus the S6 changes.

`make -B check` passes: it builds `langc` and the test tools with tcc,
runs the clang `-Wall -Wextra -Wswitch-enum -Werror` check, and runs the
gate scripts. It runs 31 examples, 19 parse refusals, 150 check
refusals, 22 kit-only refusals, 80 evals, 5 runs, 10 run refusals, 8
asmtool checks, 3 builds, 16 lower refusals, 33 build checks, 20
dispatch checks, 43 evm steps, 18 precompile steps, 13 mock rows, 15
diff rows, 24 emit regressions and the review, domain, path, fuel,
output, lower and hashing regressions, with zero failures.

The S0 review is the kit gate as generated by lang-template (retainer-lang
K1 to K4d): 19 examples, 13 parse refusals, 57 check refusals, 55 evals,
11 lower refusals and 28 build checks. The S1 review changes only
docs/PLAN.md.

The S2 review ports the sealed-lang S2 to S4 kernel (sealed-lang bc8e168,
then the S3 and S4 commits). The port adds 6 examples, 2 parse refusals,
27 check refusals, 6 evals, 3 lower refusals, 3 build checks, the 18
precompile steps of `test/precompile.sh` and the path regressions of
`test/pathtool.c`. It removes the sample domain example and its check row.
They check interval paths, the Kan operations, HITs with path
constructors, folds with path cases, Trunc1, Trunc0 and Quot, and the
precompiles 0x01, 0x06, 0x07 and 0x08 on the chain. Refusals cover path
ends, UIP and K, face systems, transport lines, missing path cases and a
path or a loop at a word position.

The S2 review also adds 8 check refusals and 4 parse refusals for the
Prop-like forms (USER ruling S1). Refusals cover `Prop` and `SProp` as a
type and as a defined name, four irrelevance marker spellings, refl
between two non-convertible elements of Trunc1 A, Trunc0 A and an
isProp-typed type, and a path of paths by refl between two different
loops. Mutants M1 to M5, M3b and M4b are killed (7 of 7, docs/STATUS.md
"## S2").

The S3 review prunes the base span (PLAN section 4). Guest mode, the
default, refuses the kit names, the sealed-lang base ops natDiv, natMod,
hash, ecrecover, ecAdd, ecMul, ecPairing and addrToU256, the Nat and
word literals, and the `state` and `event` declarations (REFUSE_KIT).
`langc --kit` is the kit mode of the kit rows: 24 examples, 80 check
refusals, 17 parse refusals and 61 evals. The table SIGNATURE reserves
the primitive and defined names of the design signature and checks the
slot types (REFUSE_SIG). The review adds 22 REFUSE_KIT and 6 REFUSE_SIG
check refusals, the guest example `signature` and its eval, and the
kit-only assertion: each REFUSE_KIT row gives `ok` under `--kit`.
Mutants K1 to K7, K2b and K4b are killed (9 of 9, 50 admissions,
docs/STATUS.md "## S3").

The S4 review adds Orbit and the dependent eliminator (PLAN section 8,
USER ruling 2026-10-17). The kernel declares Orbit and orbitProj
directly after `act`, and `elim P c1 .. cn x` eliminates Sum A B and
Unit into a motive. The review adds the guest example `orbit` (isSet
Choice by elim, the fold of Orbit into Choice) and its 3 evals, 13 check
refusals and the lower refusal `orbit-word`. Refusals cover a program
Orbit or orbitProj, Orbit with no act, an Orbit fold with no isSet case,
a false isSet case, a quotEq path with a wrong witness, elim with a
wrong case type, case count, scrutinee or motive domain, a motive into
Prop, elim on a Path (no K), Unit eta, and an Orbit value at a word
position. Mutants G0, G1, O1, E1, E1b, E2, E3, E4, F1, P1, P1b and N1b
are killed (12 of 14, 13 admissions). N1 meets a second refusal class,
and L1 is an equivalent mutant for its row (docs/STATUS.md "## S4").

The S5 review ports Glue, ua, uaBeta and hcomp at U from coin-lang
(44c917f, 91d7091 and 34acff5, PLAN section 8). Glue, glue and unglue
are guest formers (F17), ua and the equivalences are domain
definitions, and Fin is internal (O23). The review adds the kit
examples `equiv`, `glue` and `univ`, 10 evals, 15 check refusals (16
new rows; `hcomp-universe` goes out, because hcomp at Type 0 is now a
Glue type), the lower refusal `glue-word` and 10 path regression rows.
Refusals cover a Fin literal out of range, a failed Fin case, a Glue
branch that is not an equivalence, a Glue system that does not cover
its face, a Glue boundary mismatch, unglue on a term of another type, a
used tube binder, ua of an equivalence equal to refl, uaBeta on a
neutral equivalence, hcomp at U with a wrong cap, hcomp above Type 0, ua
on a non-equivalence, a Glue at Prop, two isEquiv proofs or two ua
proofs that are not convertible, and an hcomp at a word position.
Mutants M1, M2 and M4 to M9 are killed (8 of 9, 7 admissions). M3 meets
a second refusal class (PATH_END) (docs/STATUS.md "## S5").

The S6 review adds indexed families with path constructors (HIIT, F18)
and inductive-inductive blocks with path constructors (QIIT, F19) as new
work (PLAN section 8). Size, Bag, Charter and Ratified are domain
families. The review adds the guest examples `hiit` and `qiit`, 5 evals,
3 check refusals and the fronttool domain rows. Refusals cover a
constructor of an indexed family with no result or with a wrong head, an
end of a path constructor at another index, a block header that names a
later sort, Prop in an index telescope, a fold that omits a squash case
(of a family and of a block), a block fold with one target too few or
one argument too many, and a block fold whose parameters do not inhabit
the telescope of a sort. Mutants M3b, HI1, HI2, BO, BOb, FA, SR, CF and FS
are killed (9 of 9). BO and BOb are code kills, and FA needed a second
run with the new row `block-fold-extra` (docs/STATUS.md "## S6").
